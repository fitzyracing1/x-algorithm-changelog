# New semantic-ID candidate source (off by default)

- **New candidate source `SidSource`** (`home-mixer/sources/sid_source.rs`) that retrieves posts through a new `SidRetrievalClient` (seeded with up to `SidSourceMaxSeeds` post IDs taken from the viewer's signals via `post_signal_ids`). It is gated by `EnableSidSource` (default off) with `SidSourceMaxResults` = 800, `SidSourceMaxSeeds` = 50, `SidSourceMaxPerSeed` = 100 and `SidSourceMinPrefixDepth` = 3.
- Removes the `TestBoost` and `TestCap` params.
