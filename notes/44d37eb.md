# Ranking scorer rewritten as a shared `xai-value-model` crate

- **`home-mixer/scorers/ranking_scorer.rs` (1,255 lines) is deleted.** It's replaced by a 114-line `home-mixer/scorers/value_model.rs` that maps feature-switch params into a `ValueModelWeights` struct and calls `fuse_heads` from a new top-level crate, `xai-value-model/` (`scoring.rs`, `weights.rs`, `inputs.rs`, …).
- In this version, home-mixer's `weights_for` hard-codes `enable_author_diversity: false`, `oon_rescore_in_network_replies_retweets: false` and `multiplier_pre_offset: false`.
- 22 params leave `home-mixer/params/param.rs`. Eleven of them (author diversity, the OON weight factors, `MultiplierPreOffset`, `WeightPerturbation*`, …) are still defined in `vm-ranker/params.rs`. The other eleven aren't defined as params anywhere else, including `EnableVMRanker`, `CachedPostsReuseWeightedScore`, `EnableMultiplicativePostUnexplored` and the `VMRankerDpp*` settings.
- `VMRankerClusterId` changes from `"Experiment3"` to `"Experiment6"`.
