# New "multi-risk" ads blender

- **New `MultiRiskAdsBlender`** (`home-mixer/ads/multi_risk_blender.rs`). It places ads by the brand-safety verdict of the surrounding organic posts. It counts safe, medium-risk and high-risk posts, caps ad slots at half the safe posts (and half the medium-risk posts for the medium path), keeps the requested ad spacing, and records stats when ads can't be served (`no_ads`, `too_few_posts`, …). This commit doesn't make it the default (see Sep 1).
- `viewer_muted_keyword_filter` and the `response_stats` side effect get small additions, and a `strato_context` helper is added.
- The Brazil election list changes from 2,328 to 2,315 accounts.
