Sep 30, 2026 · 77d431a · 69 files, +3,306/−1,489

Cold-start boost for small accounts retuned and Thompson sampling enabled

feature flagslistsparams

This sync changes the defaults of the author cold-start mechanism (home-mixer/scorers/author_cold_start.rs). Per request, it picks one eligible fresh post from a smaller account and raises its score to whatever score sits at a fixed rank, which is index 15 given ColdStartSlotMin = 15 and ColdStartSlotMax = 16.

  • ColdStartFollowerCap 1000 → 50000: authors with up to 50k followers are now eligible.
  • ColdStartImpressionThreshold 1000 → 200: the post must have fewer than 200 Home impressions.
  • ColdStartMaxPostAgeSecs 172800 → 7200: the post must be under 2 hours old (was 48h).
  • LowImpressionsMaxPositionRatio 0.85 → 0.97: candidates from deeper in the ranked list can qualify.
  • EnableColdStartThompsonSampling now on by default. Instead of taking the highest-scored eligible post, the scorer samples a Beta(0.75 + likes, 49.25 + impressions − likes) reward per post, keeps the top ColdStartTsTopK (2), and boosts the higher-scored of those.
  • PhoenixColdStartMaxResults 0 → 200: the Phoenix cold-start retrieval path is switched on.
  • The Brazil election list goes from 2,786 to 2,795 accounts.
