Vertical video carousel added to For You (off by default)

candidate hydratorscandidate sourcesconstantsfiltersnumeric tweaksparams
  • New flag EnableVideoCarousel (default off), plus VideoCarouselPosition = 6 and VideoCarouselFatigueMinutes = 60.
  • When enabled on a For You request, the pipeline sets aside up to VIDEO_CAROUSEL_EXTRA_CANDIDATES = 30 extra video candidates (top by predicted video-open). The new VideoCarouselFilter and util/video_carousel.rs then pick 3–5 non-reply videos whose aspect ratio is below 1.0 (the code's is_vertical check), ordered by predicted video-open probability. No carousel is shown if fewer than 3 qualify.
  • New video_aspect_ratio_hydrator supplies the aspect ratio. FEED_MODULE_SLOTS 4 → 5.
  • New ads/drops.rs. Large Phoenix training/model changes in the same sync.
