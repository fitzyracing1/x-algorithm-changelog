The other eleven aren't defined as params anywhere else, including EnableVMRanker, CachedPostsReuseWeightedScore, EnableMultiplicativePostUnexplored and the VMRankerDpp* settings.
  • VMRankerClusterId changes from "Experiment3" to "Experiment6".
